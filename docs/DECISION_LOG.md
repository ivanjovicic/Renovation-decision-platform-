# Decision Log

## D-001 — Validation-first, not build-first
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Full MVP implementation is blocked until R0 evidence/taxonomy gates pass.  
**Reason:** The hardest risk is data comparability and user decision value, not software engineering.

## D-002 — Bathroom renovation is the first canonical vertical
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Start with complete bathroom renovation in Belgrade.  
**Reason:** High value, multi-trade, common hidden scope, yet bounded enough for a serious taxonomy.

## D-003 — Independent decision layer, not contractor marketplace
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Contractor discovery/leads are downstream.  
**Reason:** Daibau and Ne Radi Sam already occupy marketplace/discovery; two-sided liquidity would increase early risk.

## D-004 — AI is not the price source
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** AI extracts, maps and explains. Deterministic/versioned evidence computes benchmarks and estimates.  
**Reason:** Local price credibility is the moat and must be auditable.

## D-005 — Compare scope before price
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Quote comparison must first align canonical work items, inclusions/exclusions, quantities, material responsibility, VAT and logistics.  
**Reason:** Cheapest total is meaningless when scopes differ.

## D-006 — Do not score contractors with an opaque winner score
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Score/describe document clarity and verified facts, not contractor quality by a black-box number.  
**Reason:** Avoid false precision, bias and reputational/defamation risk.

## D-007 — Budget lifecycle is Estimated -> Committed -> Invoiced -> Paid -> Forecast
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Project control must distinguish these states and first-class change orders.  
**Reason:** “Spent vs budget” misses contractual commitments and cost drift.

## D-008 — One-time project pricing preferred over pure consumer subscription
**Status:** hypothesis  
**Decision:** Test a free decision layer + one-time project/Decision Report purchase before monthly subscription.  
**Reason:** Renovation is episodic; HouseLog provides a relevant regional signal for one-time project pricing.

## D-009 — Leads and commerce must not bias benchmark results
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Sponsored/affiliate/lead partners are separated from benchmark calculation and clearly disclosed.  
**Reason:** Trust is more valuable than short-term monetization.

## D-010 — Financing and subsidy features are adjacencies, not MVP
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Add contextual readiness/checklists later; do not build a loan broker or subsidy portal first.  
**Reason:** banks and the state already own authoritative eligibility; KojiDaBiram already targets the subsidy discovery wedge.

## D-011 — Add post-purchase / pre-renovation acquisition research
**Status:** research track  
**Decision:** Test a Renovation Readiness Report with recent apartment buyers and real-estate partners after core quote proof.  
**Reason:** RGZ shows strong apartment transaction value and Belgrade concentration, creating a high-intent acquisition moment.

## D-012 — No broad retail scraping in V1
**Status:** accepted  
**Decision:** Use reference SKU samples and later authorized partner feeds/APIs.  
**Reason:** price volatility, terms-of-use risk and data-ops burden can distract from the core decision asset.
